Wiring Legend & Schema

L - Live Wire (Red)

Carries current from mains supply to the switch input. From the switch output, it continues to the 'L' terminal of the 16A socket only when switched ON.

N - Neutral Wire (Black)

Completes the electrical circuit. Connects directly from the main Neutral supply line to the 'N' terminal of the 16A socket.

E - Earth Wire (Green)

Provides safety protection. Connects from the 'E' terminal of the 16A socket directly to the Ground earth symbol.

Connection Steps

  1. Live Supply Connection: Connect the Red Line wire from the main distribution board to the input terminal of the single pole switch.
  2. Switch Control Wire: Run a Red wire from the output terminal of the single pole switch to the L terminal of the 16A socket.
  3. Neutral Connection: Run a Black wire directly from the Neutral supply line to the N terminal of the socket.
  4. Earth Connection: Run a Green wire from the E terminal of the socket to a reliable Earth grounding rod/symbol.
